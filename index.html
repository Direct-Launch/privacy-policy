1. What we collect

When you use the contact form, we collect your first name, last name, email address, phone number, the subject you select, and the message you write.

When you email, call or message us, we keep that correspondence and the contact details you used.

When you become a client, we keep the information needed to deliver and bill for the work — contact details, business details, billing information, and any account credentials or content you choose to share with us for the project.

Automatically, when you browse, our hosting provider records standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited and the time of the visit. This is normal server logging and is used for security and to keep the site running.

We do not knowingly collect information from children, and we do not collect special category data (such as health or political opinions) through this website.

3. Who else is involved

We use a small number of service providers who may process data on our behalf, or who receive data because their technology runs on this site:

20i — UK web hosting. Stores the website and its server logs.

Google — Google Fonts are served from Google’s servers, which means Google receives your IP address when a page loads. Google Site Kit is installed, which can connect Google Analytics and Search Console. Google Workspace is used for email.

StackMail / SendGrid — email delivery.

Trustpilot, LinkedIn, WhatsApp — links and widgets on our pages. If you interact with these, you are also subject to their own privacy policies.

visibilitykit.ai — a third-party script currently present on the site. We are reviewing what it does and will update this policy accordingly.

Some of these providers are based outside the UK. Where personal data is transferred internationally, it is protected by the UK’s approved safeguards, such as the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

4. How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become work: up to 12 months, then deleted.

Client records: for the duration of the relationship, then up to 6 years after the final invoice, to meet HMRC record-keeping requirements.

Server logs: as retained by our host, typically a short rolling period.

Marketing consent records: until you withdraw consent, plus a short period to evidence the withdrawal.

5. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

Access the personal data we hold about you

Correct anything inaccurate

Erase it, where there is no overriding reason for us to keep it

Restrict or object to how we use it

Portability — receive it in a transferable format

Withdraw consent at any time, where consent is the basis we rely on

To exercise any of these, email eusef@directlaunch.co.uk. We will respond within one month. There is no charge.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office — ico.org.uk — or call 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to put it right first.

6. Security

The site is served over HTTPS, and access to client systems is restricted to the people who need it. No online service can be guaranteed completely secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, and we will tell you and the ICO without undue delay if a breach occurs that is likely to affect your rights.
