1. What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file a website stores on your device. It lets the site remember things between pages and visits. Similar technologies — local storage, pixels, embedded scripts — do comparable jobs, and this policy covers those too.

2. The categories we use

Strictly necessary — required for the site to function: keeping your session, security, remembering form state, and load balancing. These do not require your consent because the site cannot work without them. Set by WordPress and by our host, 20i.

Analytics — help us understand which pages are visited and how people move through the site, so we can improve it. Google Site Kit is installed on this site, which can connect Google Analytics. These are not essential, and we should only set them with your consent.

Third-party content and functionality — some features load from other companies, and those companies may set their own cookies or receive your IP address:

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies of our own.

3. Your consent choice

When you first visit, a banner asks whether you’re happy for us to use analytics cookies. Analytics cookies are switched off by default and stay off unless you click “Accept & Close.” Clicking Decline (or simply closing the banner without accepting) keeps them off. Your choice is remembered for a year so you’re not asked again on every visit, and you can change your mind at any time by clearing your browser’s cookies for this site, which will show the banner again.

If you’d like to know what’s been collected, or want it deleted, contact eusef@directlaunch.co.uk.

4. Managing cookies yourself

Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies:

Chrome — Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies

Safari — Settings → Privacy

Firefox — Settings → Privacy & Security

Edge — Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Blocking all cookies may stop parts of this or other sites working. To opt out of Google Analytics across all sites, Google provides a browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

5. Changes

We will update this policy as the site changes — in particular once the consent mechanism described in section 3 is live. The “last updated” date shows when it last changed.
